My role on AI research

On computational work I contribute as the clinical domain expert, not the modeller. That means defining the diagnostic reference standard, doing the histopathological annotation, judging whether a cohort reflects real disease presentation, and reading model errors for clinical consequence. The modelling itself is done by computational collaborators.

It is a deliberate division of labour, and the clinical half is the half that decides whether a model's output means anything. More on that, and on the governance side of it, here.
